# Flux Browser privacy policy

Flux Browser is developed by [World Marketing Labs](https://www.worldmarketinglabs.com/en).

Flux Browser stores your profile on the device. It can contain settings, bookmarks, browsing history, open tabs, cookies, cache and a diagnostic log. The log rotates at 1 MB and can contain paths or browsing information. Flux Browser has no telemetry of its own and does not automatically send your profile or logs to its developer.

Websites receive normal connection data and can store cookies or request permissions. Submitted searches go to the provider you select. When the optional daily background is enabled, New Tab contacts Bing for the image and credit. Microsoft distributes and updates WebView2 under its [privacy statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement).

Private windows do not add history or restore their tabs after restarting. Files, captures and bookmarks you explicitly save may remain on the device. Websites and network services can observe their own connections.

Use Settings → Privacy, search and services to clear browsing data. To delete the full GitHub-build profile, close Flux Browser and remove `%LOCALAPPDATA%\FluxBrowser` or the directory selected using `FLUXBROWSER_PROFILE`. Uninstalling the EXE preserves the profile. Review attachments before sending a report to GitHub.

Privacy contact · [contact@worldmarketinglabs.com](mailto:contact@worldmarketinglabs.com). Report security issues privately through [GitHub](https://github.com/cbatista96/FluxBrowser/security/advisories/new).
